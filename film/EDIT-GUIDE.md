# Edit guide

Everything needed to assemble the film. Shots are grouped by sequence in `scenes/`, sound in
`sounds/`.

1. `scenes/00-opening.md` — 4 shots, ends on the title card
2. `scenes/01-helicopter.md` — 9 shots

## The rule that matters most

**Every French moment has a waiting beat, and the wait is the lesson.** Michel points, asks, and
the shot holds in silence. Give a child 2–3 seconds to answer out loud before cutting to the
answer. If you trim those pauses to tighten the pace, the film stops teaching and becomes a man
saying French words.

There are eight of them: hélicoptère, voleur, caméra, patrouille, menottes, radio, sirène, policier.

## Added in post, never rendered

The CCTV timestamp · the `לא ללחוץ` label on the red button · callout graphics over the
investigation explanation · Hebrew subtitles · titles and the end card.

No Hebrew text was ever generated by the video model, deliberately.

## Pacing

0.5–1.0 s of silence before every punchline. Smash cut out of 1H. Hold the freeze after the thief
waves at the camera in 2E. Let "סלט" sit in complete silence. Drop the music entirely under
"זה הראש שלו".

Fast enough to hold a five-year-old, never so fast that a joke has no room to land.
