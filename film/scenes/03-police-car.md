## Edit notes

**Add the `לא ללחוץ` label on the red button in post** — never render it.

3E, 3F and 3D share one flat delivery. **The repetition is the joke** — do not vary the timing.
3I → 3K is the setup and the break: hold the hesitation on the hovering finger as long as it stays
funny, then cut the press hard.

**3M uses one take processed two ways** — dry inside the car, then the same take with PA processing
heard from outside. That is the whole gag.

**Radio processing (3-fr-radio, 3O):** same chain as the megaphone — filtered EQ, compression, mild
distortion, thin static. Michel must stay recognizable. Layer the police-radio effect underneath.

**Sound:** one switch click per press, escalating. Full chaos layer on 3L. Handcuff rattle on the
menottes shot. Sudden total silence after 3M.
