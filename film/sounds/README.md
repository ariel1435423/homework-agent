## Voice processing — this is post, not generation

Two effects in the film are **processed dialogue**, not sound effects. Michel's clean voice is
generated once and then treated:

**Megaphone** (1F, 1H) and **radio / PA** (3-fr-radio, 3M exterior, 3O):
high-pass EQ → reduced bass → compression → mild distortion → metallic tone → slight echo.
Then layer the matching effect bed underneath.

He must still clearly sound like Michel. If the processing makes him unrecognizable, back it off —
the joke only works if the audience knows it is him.

Shot 3M uses **one take processed two ways**: dry inside the car, PA-treated from outside.

## Mix

Five stems, always separate: dialogue · ambience · Foley · SFX · music.
Dialogue stays clear at all times; music and ambience duck under it.
Never bake rotor, siren, wind, music or megaphone into the dialogue track.
