# Cut plan

The full film costs roughly 1,900 credits to render. The available balance is well under that, so
the plan below trims scope to fit. Every educational goal in the brief survives; the cuts fall on
repetition and on render tier, not on content.

Real cost per shot: **~8 credits/second** for a lipsynced talking-head shot (video ~4.5/s plus the
sync pass ~3.6/s), **~2.5 credits/second** for B-roll on the mini model at 720p.

## Shots converted to VO over B-roll

1J, 2G, 3P, 4B, 5B, 5D

Michel's voice plays over footage of the helicopter, the police car, the CCTV monitor and the city
instead of a lipsynced talking head. This drops the sync pass entirely and moves the shot to the
cheaper model. The brief already asks for B-roll over constant lipsync, so this follows it rather
than fighting it. Michel stays on camera for the anchor beats — 5C, 5E and 5F are untouched.

## CCTV shots dropped to 480p

2C, 2E, 2H

Surveillance footage is supposed to look degraded. This is the one cut that improves the film.
